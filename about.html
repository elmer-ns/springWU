    Frequently asked questions

    • What is Veii?

      Veii, pronounced [ˈweː.jiː] or [ˈvɛː.ji], was an ancient etruscan city in Italy. It is also a multi-platform modular UI/Application frameworks written in Rust.

    • Why was Veii made?

      Mostly because I'm tired of most other mainstream frameworks and languages. Veii explores unusual and non-standard ideas to create something wholly new.

    • Is Veii available?

      Not yet! If you look at the "Get Started" page, you can see installation instructions and find a github link, but I have simply used to an advanced technique called "lying". The basic functionality exists, but there is much more work to do until Veii is usable at scale.
